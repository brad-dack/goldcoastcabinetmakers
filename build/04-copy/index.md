Hero sub-headline (`pages.home.subheadline`):

Describe what you would like built, and we pass your enquiry to a cabinet maker near you who can quote it.

1. `h2`: Start with what you actually need

2. `p`: Most people looking for a cabinet maker on the Gold Coast are in one of three situations. Each is a different kind of job, and working out which is yours gets you a more useful quote.

3. `h3`: You want a new kitchen

4. `p`: That might mean fresh cabinets, a changed layout, a replacement benchtop, or the lot. In most houses the kitchen is the largest cabinet job there is, and its price turns on the layout, the materials, and how much has to be removed before work starts. [Kitchens on the Gold Coast](kitchens-gold-coast.html)

5. `h3`: You want storage built to fit a room

6. `p`: Built-in wardrobes, a walk-in robe, a laundry, shelving or a media wall. Each is built to the exact space, so expect the cabinet maker to measure up before giving a price.

7. `h3`: You are not sure what you need yet

8. `p`: Tell us about the room and what bothers you about it now. From that, a cabinet maker can work out whether it calls for a kitchen, some joinery or a smaller fix. The form below accepts whatever detail you have, a little or a lot.

9. `h2`: Joinery on the Gold Coast

10. `p`: Joinery is the built-in timberwork in a home that is made to measure rather than bought off the shelf: built-in wardrobes, shelving and bookcases, media walls, TV and entertainment units, window seats, study nooks, laundry cabinets, bar and butler's pantry fit-outs, and vanity cabinets. On the Gold Coast the words joiner and cabinet maker are used for much the same trade, and many businesses do both.

11. `p`: What makes a joinery job different from buying furniture is that it is designed for one space. That means the cabinet maker needs the measurements, what the piece is for, and how it meets the walls, floor and ceiling. A rough sketch with dimensions is a good start.

12. `p`: Tell us what you want built and roughly where it goes, and we pass it to a local cabinet maker who does joinery.

13. `h2`: Wardrobes and built-in storage

14. `p`: Built-in wardrobes are made to the width and height of the wall, so there is no gap above or beside them, and the inside is planned around what you store: hanging space, drawers, shelves and shoe storage. A walk-in robe is the same idea for a whole small room.

15. `p`: The main choices are the doors (hinged, sliding or none), the finish, and the layout inside. If you know how much hanging space you need and how many drawers, say so in your enquiry.

16. `image`: `images/wardrobe-joinery.jpg`, illustrative, no caption. Alt text at T24.

17. `h2`: What a good quote covers

18. `p`: Two quotes for what sounds like one job can differ simply because they include different work. Whichever cabinet maker gets in touch, these questions are worth putting to them:

19. `ul`:
    - What is included: the cabinets, the benchtop, removing the old ones, installation
    - The material used for the cabinets, and whether cut edges get sealed
    - Who provides and installs the parts that are not cabinets, like the benchtop and appliances
    - Whether the time quoted covers making the cabinets as well as the work in your home
    - How changes are handled if the scope shifts partway through
    - Whether your job needs a written contract, and the QBCC licence number they hold

20. `p`: The detail on contracts, deposits and licensing, and published price ranges for Gold Coast kitchens, are on the [kitchens](kitchens-gold-coast.html) page.

21. `h2`: Where we cover

22. `p`: The Gold Coast, from the northern suburbs down to the southern end, including Southport, Nerang and Burleigh Heads. If you are somewhere else in south-east Queensland, tell us where, and we will try our best!

23. `h2`: What to include when you enquire

24. `p`: Detail helps: a specific enquiry gets a more useful quote. Worth including:

25. `ul`:
    - Which room it is, and its rough size
    - New work, a replacement, or a repair
    - Whether you have a layout, drawings or photos, or just an idea
    - Any materials you have in mind
    - Anything tricky about access, timing or the room's condition

26. `p`: Fill in what you can. The cabinet maker will look at the room before settling on a price.

27. `h2`: Common questions

28. `faqs`:
    - Q: What is the difference between a joiner and a cabinet maker? A: On the Gold Coast the two are used for much the same trade: made-to-measure cabinets and built-in timberwork. You will also see it written as one word, cabinetmaker, and cabinet builder is used for the same trade. Some businesses lean towards kitchens and some towards wardrobes and other joinery. Describe your job and it goes to someone who does that kind of work.
    - Q: How does a custom kitchen differ from a flat-pack one? A: Flat-pack cabinets are made in set sizes and assembled on site from parts. Custom cabinets are built to your room's measurements. The right choice comes down to your room, layout and budget, and a cabinet maker can explain which fits where. More on [kitchens on the Gold Coast](kitchens-gold-coast.html).
    - Q: Do cabinet makers in Queensland need a licence? A: For any building work valued over $3,300, yes: Queensland requires a QBCC licence. Get the number before you commit to the job, and confirm it with the QBCC. The details are on the [kitchens](kitchens-gold-coast.html) page.
    - Q: Do you give prices? A: We do not quote or set prices. The [kitchens](kitchens-gold-coast.html) page shows price ranges for Gold Coast kitchens that other publishers have put out, with where each comes from. Your price comes from the cabinet maker once they have seen the room.
    - Q: What happens after I send an enquiry? A: We pass your details to a local cabinet maker who can quote the job, and they get in touch with you directly. The enquiry is paid for by the cabinet maker, not by you. The [About page](about.html) has the full account.
